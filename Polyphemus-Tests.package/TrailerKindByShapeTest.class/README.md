How an image writes the trailers of its methods, told from the methods themselves where its CompiledMethod does not tell (#118). Measured on 2026-11-16 over every method of six images: with variable trailers (candle, Pharo 10 and 11) each method ends in a byte naming one of the trailer kinds, 100 of 100; with fixed ones (Pharo 12, 13, 14) the last byte is a byte of a pointer, any of them, a known kind for about 22 in 100. The reading here is a Pharo 12 image, whose trailers are fixed.
